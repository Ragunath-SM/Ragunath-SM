## About Me

I am Ragunath S M, an AI Web Developer and R&amp;D Lead at Navabrind IT Solutions, based in Villupuram, Tamil Nadu. I enjoy turning business ideas into useful web products, automation workflows, and AI-assisted tools that are clear, scalable, and easy for people to use.

My strongest work sits at the intersection of backend engineering, AI-enabled application development, API integrations, and full-stack product delivery. I work confidently with PHP ecosystems such as Laravel and CodeIgniter, enterprise platforms such as Pimcore and Odoo, and modern frontend stacks using JavaScript, TypeScript, HTML, and CSS.

I believe in building with curiosity, discipline, and a product mindset: understand the problem, design the flow, ship the solution, measure what matters, and keep improving.
